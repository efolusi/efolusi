# Vision: efolusi.com

**The public face of PT. Efolusi Dunia Teknologi.**

## The problem it solves

A studio shipping many products under one identity needs one place that tells the whole story: what the products are, who the company is, how the $EFO ecosystem fits together, and how to get in touch. Without it, each product floats alone and the brand compounds nothing.

## What it is

The open-source marketing site for Efolusi: product portfolio, the $EFO ecosystem token, company, careers, brand assets, and the contact and newsletter forms. Built entirely on the [Meridian design system](https://github.com/efolusi/meridian), so it is also the design system's public showcase.

## Why open-source

The site carries no secrets and no moat; opening it demonstrates the studio's build quality, showcases Meridian in production, and lets anyone study how a multi-product brand site is assembled.

## Where this fits in Efolusi

Open-source, the portfolio's front door. Every product links back here; here is where a visitor learns the products belong to one company with one thesis: open where it helps, closed where it counts, solving problems the giants overlook. See the company [VISION.md](../000/VISION.md).

## What success looks like

A visitor lands on efolusi.com, understands within a minute what the studio builds and why, and leaves into a product, the newsletter, or the contact form.
